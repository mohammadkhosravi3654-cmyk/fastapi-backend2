# Revenue Engine

A practical service-selling layer for AI-assisted microservices.

## Operating model
1. Capture a paid order.
2. Validate the requested deliverable and inputs.
3. Generate the deliverable with AI-assisted tooling.
4. Quality-check the result.
5. Deliver the final artifact.
6. Record status, revenue, delivery time, and repeat-sale opportunity.

## Starter offers
- Product listing rewrite — $5
- 10 social captions — $7
- CV / resume optimization — $10
- Landing page copy — $15
- Competitor research brief — $20
- Custom AI-assisted task — from $25

## Guardrails
Revenue must come from legitimate services or products. No fraud, spam, fake reviews, impersonation, policy evasion, or unauthorized access to payment accounts.
